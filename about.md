# About the AI Education Coalition

The AI Education Coalition is a collaborative effort among regional districts to share resources, develop common frameworks, and support educators in integrating artificial intelligence into teaching, learning, and district operations.

## Goals
- Promote safe, ethical, and effective AI use  
- Share district-created resources  
- Support professional learning  
- Build a regional AI knowledge base  
- Provide examples of AI-supported instruction  

## Contact
For questions or contributions, please reach out to the coalition coordination team.
